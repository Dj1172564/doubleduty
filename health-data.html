Consumer health data notice

Effective November 7, 2026. This notice is for people in Washington State and other places with consumer health data laws, and applies to everyone.

Health data the apps handle

Pregnancy and labor information (due date, contraction timings, water breaking, birth plan choices, journal entries) and baby care information (feeding, pumping, diapers, sleep, growth).

Why

Only to show it back to you: timers, summaries, charts, reminders you turn on, PDFs you choose to export and, in Double Duty Newborn, sharing with people you invite.

Where it goes

It stays on your devices and, for Double Duty Newborn, in your own iCloud account. We don't collect it on our servers, and we don't share or sell it to anyone. Apple processes iCloud data on your behalf under Apple's privacy policy.

Your rights

You can see, export and delete your data in the apps at any time (Settings → Reset all data in Double Duty, Clear all data in Double Duty Newborn). Because we don't hold a copy, there is nothing for us to send or delete on our side, but you can contact us with any question through the support page.
