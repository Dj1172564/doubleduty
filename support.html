Support

Questions, bugs or ideas for Double Duty or Double Duty Newborn? Send us a message here (a free GitHub account is needed for now; an email address is coming soon). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

In labor or worried about you or your baby? The apps don't give medical advice. Call your care team, your baby's doctor or emergency services.

Common questions

Does Double Duty need an account? No. Nothing to sign up for.

Is labor timing free? Yes, always. Double Duty Plus is a one-time purchase for the full birth plan, photo cards, journal extras and personalization.

How do I restore Plus? Settings → Double Duty Plus → Restore purchases.

How does sharing work in Double Duty Newborn? Family tab → Share. Your partner accepts the iCloud invite and sees changes live.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Reset all data (Double Duty) or Clear all data (Double Duty Newborn).
