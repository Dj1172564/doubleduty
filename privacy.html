Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Covers Double Duty: Contraction Timer and Double Duty Newborn: Baby Log (the "apps").

The short version: what you enter stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers that receive your data, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your information.

What the apps store

  • Double Duty: contraction times and notes, pauses, your due date, birth plan, journal entries and photos you add, hospital bag lists, care team names and phone numbers, recipient lists for announcements, breathing settings and app preferences.
  • Double Duty Newborn: your babies' names and birth dates, feeds, pumping, diapers, sleep, tummy time, growth entries, notes, monthly photo cards and app preferences.

Where it is kept

  • On your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch, widgets and Lock Screen (Live Activities) where you use them.
  • Double Duty Newborn syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). If you invite a partner or caregiver, the shared records are kept in iCloud and visible only to the people you invite. Apple stores this data under your iCloud account; we can't read it.
  • Double Duty's partner handoff is a file you choose to send yourself, for example through AirDrop or Messages.

Contacts, photos and notifications

  • Contacts: when you pick people for an announcement list, the app saves only the names and numbers you choose, on your iPhone. It doesn't read your whole address book.
  • Photos: photos you add stay in the app. Location data is removed from photos used for photo cards. The app only asks to save to your library when you tap Save.
  • Notifications: reminders (for example the contraction call rule or month-day photo reminders) are scheduled on your device and only when you turn them on.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, including consumer health data.

If we ever add ads or anything that changes this, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details first, and ask for any permission the system requires.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all data in Double Duty or Settings → Clear all data in Double Duty Newborn, or delete the app. For Double Duty Newborn's iCloud data, you can also remove it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Stopping a share removes others' access.

Children

The apps are for parents and caregivers, not for children to use.
